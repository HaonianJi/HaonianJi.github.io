About

Building adaptive AI for open-ended problems.

My research explores self-evolving agents, multimodal reasoning, AI alignment, and optimization. As part of this broader agenda, I contribute to RSI-Exam through executable research tasks, large-scale agent evaluation, and evaluation-infrastructure auditing.

Before starting my Ph.D., I was a research intern at the AIMING Lab, where I worked on AI alignment and multimodal foundation models. Earlier, I researched large models for autonomous driving at CIDI with Dr. Zhaorun Chen.
