About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Computer and Artificial Intelligence, Southwest Jiaotong University. My advisor is Hongmei Chen.

During the 2024-2025 period, I am engaged in a remote research internship at the AIMING Lab, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, under the supervision of Prof. Huaxiu Yao, focusing on AI alignment. I am contributing to multiple projects, with two co-authored papers (MJ-Video and GLIMPSE) accepted to NIPS 2025.
