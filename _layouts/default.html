Haonian Ji

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Prof. Huaxiu Yao. My research interests include AI alignment, optimization, and machine learning.

I received my bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the School of Computer and Artificial Intelligence, Southwest Jiaotong University, in 2026. There, I worked on evolutionary optimization with Prof. Hongmei Chen and led a national-level undergraduate innovation project on large-model-based reading assessment and early dyslexia screening.

Before starting my Ph.D., I was a remote research intern at the AIMING Lab at UNC-Chapel Hill, where I worked on AI alignment and multimodal foundation models. My recent work includes EduVisAgent, published at ICLR 2026, and MJ-Video.

Previously, during my sophomore year, I interned at CIDI, an intelligent driving company, where I conducted research on the role of large models in autonomous driving under the mentorship of Dr. Zhaorun Chen from the University of Chicago.
