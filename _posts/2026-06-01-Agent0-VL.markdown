Agent0-VL is a self-evolving vision-language agent that integrates tool use into reasoning, self-evaluation, and self-repair. Without human annotations or external reward models, it continually improves its evidence-grounded multimodal reasoning and achieves strong results on geometric problem solving and visual scientific analysis.
