This project focuses on leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) for the early screening and assessment of dyslexia. As a national-level SRTP project, it has led to two pending patents. The core of the project is a system that uses LLMs to automatically generate diagnostic assessments based on established dyslexia rules. Furthermore, the project employs LLM-driven data interpretation to analyze the root causes of dyslexia, enabling the design of targeted training exercises to improve reading abilities for various learner groups.
