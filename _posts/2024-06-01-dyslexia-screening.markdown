- Led a national-level SRTP project, resulting in two pending patents, to develop a dyslexia detection system using Large Language Models (LLMs) that automatically generates diagnostic assessments based on established dyslexia rules.
- Utilized LLM-driven data interpretation to analyze the root causes of dyslexia and design targeted training exercises to improve reading abilities across different learner groups.
